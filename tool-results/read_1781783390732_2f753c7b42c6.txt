  8620→The Maker Framework For Zero-Error Llm Execution Introduction to MAKER The MAKER Framework (Maximal Agentic
  8621→Decomposition with First-to-ahead-by-K Error correction and Red-flagging) represents a breakthrough in achieving near-
  8622→zero error rates for complex, multi-step AI operations. Developed by Cognizant AI Lab, this framework enables LLMs to
  8623→execute tasks with unprecedented reliability critical for customer care applications where errors can directly impact
  8624→customer satisfaction and business outcomes. Core Components of MAKER The MAKER Framework consists of three
  8625→interconnected pillars that work together to ensure reliable AI execution. 1. Maximal Agentic Decomposition (MAD) The
  8626→first pillar involves breaking down complex tasks into the smallest possible atomic operations. Rather than asking an AI to
  8627→process a refund, the task is decomposed into: verify customer identity, retrieve order details, check refund eligibility
  8628→against policy, calculate refund amount, verify payment method, execute refund transaction, send confirmation, and
  8629→update internal records. Each atomic operation is handled by a specialized agent, dramatically reducing the probability of
  8630→errors cascading through the system. 2. First-to-Ahead-by-K Error Correction (FAKE) The second pillar implements predictive
  8631→error detection. The system generates K possible solutions in parallel and evaluates them before execution. K=3 is the
  8632→default for standard queries, K=5 for high-stakes financial decisions, and K=7 for critical irreversible actions. The system
  8633→selects the first solution that demonstrates clear superiority in policy compliance, customer satisfaction prediction, risk
  8634→assessment alignment, and factual accuracy verification. 3. Red-Flagging System The third pillar implements real-time
  8635→anomaly detection with automatic escalation. Category A (Immediate Halt)
  8636→includes confidence scores below 60%, contradictions with knowledge base, severe customer dissatisfaction, and
  8637→policy violations. Category B (Enhanced Review) includes unusual request patterns, high-value transactions, VIP customers,
  8638→and new query types. Category C (Log and Monitor) includes slight deviations and edge cases. PARWA Implementation of
  8639→MAKER PARWA integrates MAKER at three levels: Input Processing decomposes queries into intent atoms with parallel
  8640→hypothesis generation; Decision Generation creates multiple solution paths with first-to-ahead-by-K selection; and the
  8641→
  8642→Execution Layer ensures atomic operations with rollback capabilities and complete audit trails. Performance Metrics
  8643→Without MAKER: Error rate 3-5%, escalation rate 8-12%, resolution accuracy 94%. With MAKER: Error rate 0.3-0.8%,
  8644→escalation rate 2-4%, resolution accuracy 99.2%. This represents a 6-16x improvement in error rates for complex multi-step
  8645→tasks. MAKER Configuration Options PARWA offers three MAKER configurations: Conservative Mode (K=7, 75% threshold)
  8646→for healthcare and finance; Balanced Mode (K=3-5, 60% threshold) for e-commerce and SaaS; and Efficiency Mode (K=3,
  8647→50% threshold) for high-volume low-risk queries.
