  1741→  1741→## 16. MAKER Hallucination Prevention
  1742→  1742→
  1743→  1743→### 3 Safeguards
  1744→  1744→
  1745→  1745→**Safeguard 1: Confidence Scoring**
  1746→  1746→```
  1747→  1747→MAKER assigns a confidence score to each bridge connection:
  1748→  1748→- High confidence (> 85%): "Pro plan refund → 30-day policy" (direct match)
  1749→  1749→- Medium confidence (60-85%): "Pro plan refund → similar to Business plan" (inferred)
  1750→  1750→- Low confidence (< 60%): "Pro plan refund → maybe related to annual billing" (weak)
  1751→  1751→
  1752→  1752→Only high + medium confidence bridges enter reasoning.
  1753→  1753→Low confidence bridges are flagged but NOT used.
  1754→  1754→```
  1755→  1755→
  1756→  1756→**Safeguard 2: ZeroShotValidator Gate**
  1757→  1757→```
  1758→  1758→Before MAKER's output enters reasoning:
  1759→  1759→ZeroShotValidator checks:
  1760→  1760→- Are the bridge connections logically consistent with knowledge from Node 3?
  1761→  1761→- Does any bridge contradict known facts?
  1762→  1762→- Are there alternative bridges that are more likely?
  1763→  1763→
  1764→  1764→If ZeroShotValidator flags a bridge → it's removed before reasoning uses it.
  1765→  1765→```
  1766→  1766→
  1767→  1767→**Safeguard 3: Reverse Thinking Check**
  1768→  1768→```
  1769→  1769→After reasoning uses MAKER's bridges:
  1770→  1770→Reverse Thinking verifies:
  1771→  1771→- Does the final conclusion DEPEND on low-confidence bridges?
  1772→  1772→- If we remove MAKER's bridges, does the answer change significantly?
