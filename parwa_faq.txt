PARWA Password Reset Guide

To reset your password:
1. Go to parwa.buzz/login
2. Click Forgot Password
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your inbox for the reset link
5. Click the link and set a new password

If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support at parwa.buzz.

PARWA Account Setup
1. Sign up at parwa.buzz with your business email
2. Choose your variant Starter Growth or High
3. Upload your knowledge base documents
4. Configure your support channels

PARWA Billing
Starter: 100 tickets per month email only
Growth: 500 tickets per month email plus chat
High: Unlimited tickets all channels
